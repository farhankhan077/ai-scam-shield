FEDNOW & RTP RISK RULES — AI SCAM SHIELD
Source: Federal Reserve FedNow Service Participant Rules + The Clearing House RTP Network Rules
Last Updated: 2024

===== SECTION 1: TRANSACTION VELOCITY THRESHOLDS =====

FedNow Velocity Rules (per sending account):
- LOW RISK: 1–4 transactions per hour → Normal consumer/business behavior
- MEDIUM RISK: 5–8 transactions per hour → Elevated monitoring required; apply enhanced due diligence
- HIGH RISK: 9–14 transactions per hour → Automatic hold recommended; human review required within 15 minutes
- CRITICAL: 15+ transactions per hour → Automatic block; SAR filing review mandatory

RTP Network Velocity Thresholds:
- Consumer accounts: Maximum 25 transactions per 24-hour period under standard limits
- Business accounts: Maximum 100 transactions per 24-hour period
- Velocity exceeding 80% of maximum triggers an alert to the Risk Operations Center

===== SECTION 2: TRANSACTION AMOUNT THRESHOLDS =====

FedNow Amount Risk Tiers:
- Tier 1 (Low): $0–$999 → No additional friction
- Tier 2 (Medium): $1,000–$4,999 → Standard monitoring; beneficiary verification recommended
- Tier 3 (High): $5,000–$24,999 → Enhanced due diligence; first-time beneficiary review mandatory
- Tier 4 (Critical): $25,000+ → Real-time human review required before clearing; 90-second hold permitted

Currency Transaction Report (CTR) Threshold:
- Transactions over $10,000 must be reported to FinCEN within 15 days
- Structured transactions (multiple transactions designed to avoid the $10,000 threshold) are illegal under 31 USC 5324

===== SECTION 3: NEW BENEFICIARY RULES =====

FedNow Best Practices for New Beneficiaries:
- First-time transfers to a new beneficiary over $500 should trigger a confirmation step
- First-time transfers over $2,500 should apply a 4-hour cooling-off period for consumer accounts (unless waived by customer)
- Financial institutions must maintain a list of "trusted" beneficiaries after 3 successful transactions

Account Age Considerations:
- Beneficiary account under 30 days old: HIGH risk flag; apply maximum friction
- Beneficiary account 30–90 days old: MEDIUM risk flag; enhanced monitoring
- Beneficiary account 90–365 days old: LOW additional risk from account age alone
- Beneficiary account over 365 days old: No additional risk from account age

===== SECTION 4: ACCOUNT AGE — SENDER =====

Sender Account Age Rules:
- Sender account under 14 days old: CRITICAL risk; transfers over $500 require manual approval
- Sender account 14–30 days old: HIGH risk; transfers over $2,000 require enhanced verification
- Sender account 30–90 days old: MEDIUM risk; standard enhanced monitoring applies
- Sender account over 90 days old: Normal risk evaluation applies

===== SECTION 5: TIME-OF-DAY RISK MODIFIERS =====

FedNow operates 24/7, but risk profiles vary by time:
- Business hours (8 AM–6 PM local): Normal baseline risk
- Evening (6 PM–10 PM): +10% risk modifier for consumer accounts making high-value transfers
- Late night/early morning (10 PM–6 AM): +25% risk modifier; majority of ATO fraud occurs in this window
- Weekends and federal holidays: +15% risk modifier

===== SECTION 6: FEDNOW FRAUD REPORTING REQUIREMENTS =====

Financial institutions participating in FedNow must:
1. Report suspected fraud within 24 hours to the Federal Reserve Fraud Reporting System
2. Maintain fraud detection systems with real-time monitoring capabilities
3. Implement transaction monitoring with at minimum the following controls:
   - Velocity checks (per hour and per day)
   - Amount threshold alerts
   - New beneficiary flagging
   - Account age verification
4. Provide fraud resolution within 10 business days of customer report
5. File a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) for fraud losses over $5,000

===== SECTION 7: MACHINE LEARNING MODEL REQUIREMENTS =====

FedNow guidance on AI/ML fraud detection:
- Models must be explainable — financial institutions must be able to describe why a transaction was flagged
- Model bias testing required at minimum annually
- Human-in-the-loop review required for all Critical risk decisions
- Model performance metrics must be tracked: False Positive Rate < 2%, Recall > 95% for fraud cases
- Model decisions must be logged for audit purposes for a minimum of 7 years
