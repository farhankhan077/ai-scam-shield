FRAUD REMEDIATION PLAYBOOK — AI SCAM SHIELD
Source: Risk Operations Standard Operating Procedure v4.2
Last Updated: 2024

===== SECTION 1: RISK LEVEL DEFINITIONS AND RESPONSE MATRIX =====

SAFE (Risk Score 0–39):
- Action: APPROVE automatically
- Monitoring: Standard transaction logging
- Human Review: None required
- SLA: Process within 5 seconds
- Notification: None

MEDIUM RISK (Risk Score 40–69):
- Action: Approve with enhanced monitoring; OR apply soft hold for 60 seconds
- Monitoring: Flag for next-day review by compliance team
- Human Review: Recommended within 4 hours if multiple medium-risk transactions from same account
- SLA: Decision within 60 seconds
- Notification: Send in-app notification to customer confirming transaction details

HIGH RISK (Risk Score 70–84):
- Action: HOLD FUNDS — apply a 4-hour hold pending review
- Monitoring: Immediate escalation to Tier 1 Fraud Analyst
- Human Review: Required within 30 minutes
- SLA: Decision within 30 minutes
- Notification: Call customer to verbally confirm transaction intent; send SMS alert
- Documentation: Log all decision rationale; prepare pre-SAR memo

CRITICAL RISK (Risk Score 85–100):
- Action: BLOCK transaction immediately; funds returned to sender
- Monitoring: Real-time Tier 2 Senior Analyst escalation
- Human Review: Required immediately (< 15 minutes)
- SLA: Decision within 15 minutes; customer notification within 5 minutes
- Documentation: Open SAR case; notify Compliance Officer
- Law Enforcement: Contact local FBI field office if loss > $100,000

===== SECTION 2: DECISION TREE FOR HOLD FUNDS ACTION =====

When "Hold Funds" is selected by an analyst:
1. Send customer notification: "We have temporarily held your transaction [ID] for security review. We will contact you within [X] hours."
2. Attempt verbal confirmation call (3 attempts within 30 minutes)
3. If customer confirms: Release hold; add beneficiary to verified list; log analyst override
4. If customer cannot be reached: Escalate to Tier 2
5. If customer reports fraud: Initiate full fraud case; file SAR; attempt recall
6. Hold period maximum: 4 hours for High Risk; 24 hours for Critical Risk

===== SECTION 3: ESCALATION PROCEDURES =====

Tier 1 (Fraud Analyst) Responsibilities:
- Review all High Risk (70–84) flagged transactions
- Conduct customer outreach
- Make approve/hold/escalate decision
- Document reasoning in case management system

Tier 2 (Senior Fraud Analyst) Responsibilities:
- Review all Critical Risk (85–100) flagged transactions
- Handle all Tier 1 escalations
- Authorize SAR filings
- Coordinate with law enforcement
- Issue media holds if applicable

Tier 3 (Compliance Officer / Legal) Responsibilities:
- Approve SAR filings over $25,000
- Coordinate regulatory reporting
- Handle law enforcement subpoenas
- Manage customer litigation risk

===== SECTION 4: ACTIONS AND THEIR MEANINGS =====

APPROVE:
- Transaction is cleared to process immediately
- No further action required at this time
- Standard monitoring continues
- Use when: Risk score is low, or analyst has confirmed legitimacy with customer

HOLD FUNDS:
- Transaction is temporarily suspended, pending review
- Funds remain in sender's account; transaction is not canceled
- Use when: Transaction is suspicious but not confirmed fraud; customer needs to be reached; more information is needed
- Note: Cannot hold for more than 4 hours without customer consent or legal authority

ESCALATE TO TIER 2:
- Analyst cannot make a definitive decision
- Case is transferred to a Senior Fraud Analyst immediately
- Use when: Transaction involves amounts over $25,000; customer is unresponsive; multiple accounts are involved; organized fraud ring is suspected

REJECT (Block):
- Transaction is permanently canceled and funds returned to sender
- Use when: Confirmed fraud; SAR is being filed; law enforcement has issued a hold order
- Note: Rejection must be documented with full rationale; customer notification required within 1 hour

===== SECTION 5: REGULATORY REPORTING TIMELINES =====

Suspicious Activity Report (SAR):
- Must be filed within 30 calendar days of identifying suspicious activity
- If suspect is identified, deadline is 60 days
- Minimum threshold: $5,000 for banks; $2,000 for money services businesses
- Where to file: FinCEN BSA E-Filing System

Currency Transaction Report (CTR):
- Required for any cash transaction over $10,000
- Must be filed within 15 days
- Does not apply to instant payment transfers (non-cash), but banks should be aware of structuring patterns that mirror CTR avoidance

Law Enforcement Notification:
- Emergency notification (FBI, local police) warranted for:
  - Active fraud in progress with victim still communicating with fraudster
  - Loss amounts over $100,000
  - Evidence of organized fraud ring (multiple accounts, coordinated timing)

===== SECTION 6: CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION SCRIPTS =====

Script A — Fraud Confirmation Call (Outbound):
"Hello, this is [Name] from [Bank] Fraud Prevention. I'm calling about a payment of [Amount] you recently initiated to [Beneficiary]. Can you confirm that you authorized this payment? [Pause] Did anyone ask you to make this payment? [Pause] Do you know the person you're sending money to personally?"

Script B — Hold Notification (SMS):
"[BANK ALERT] Your payment of $[Amount] to [Beneficiary] has been temporarily held for security review. We'll contact you within [X] hours. Do NOT share this code: [Code]. Reply STOP to opt out of alerts."

Script C — Release Confirmation:
"Your payment of $[Amount] to [Beneficiary] has been released and is now processing. If you did not authorize this payment, call [Fraud Hotline] immediately."
